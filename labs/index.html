MODEL AND RUNTIME

Know what the preview assumes.

Propagation: two-body Keplerian model.

Serving rule: highest elevation at or above the configured minimum elevation.

Delay: geometric one-way satellite-to-UE propagation delay; RTT is twice that value.

Runtime: the application is static and browser-based; CesiumJS and online map tiles require internet access.
