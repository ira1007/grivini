Grivini NTN Lab

Grivini NTN Lab is a UE-centric engineering visualization platform for studying satellite motion and NTN link geometry from 3GPP SIB19-style inputs.

The current v5.1 preview focuses on orbit visualization, SIB19 conversion, live geometry metrics, serving-satellite analysis, and mobility playback.
