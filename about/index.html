ABOUT GRIVINI

Engineering tools that make complex systems visible.

Grivini NTN Lab focuses on the relationship between standardized ephemeris data, orbital motion, UE-relative geometry, and observable service behavior.

01

UE-centric

The visualization starts from what the UE can see and measure: local geometry, service eligibility, delay, Doppler, and changing satellite conditions.

02

Traceable

Inputs, conversions, propagated state, visualization layers, and reported metrics remain connected in one workflow.

03

Interactive

Camera modes and mobility playback help engineers inspect behavior that is difficult to understand from static logs or tables alone.
